Privacy Policy

SwapnoPay (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects the privacy and security of merchants, customers, and users who rely on our payment, POS, accounting, and business management platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you access our website, mobile app, merchant dashboard, APIs, or related services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal information: name, email address, phone number, business name, billing address, government ID information, merchant profile details, and payment or account-related identifiers.

Transaction information: payment details, invoice data, POS sales records, transaction amounts, timestamps, ledger entries, and payout metadata.

Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, app version, language preferences, access times, error logs, and session metadata.

Telephony and SIM carrier data: Installed SIM card slot information and carrier names accessed strictly to allow merchants to route automated customer SMS invoices through their preferred SIM card. Android system refers to this permission as 'Make and manage phone calls', but SwapnoPay NEVER initiates, intercepts, records, or manages telephone calls, nor accesses user call logs.

Microphone and Voice data: Audio input captured strictly when the merchant or customer voluntarily taps the microphone to interact with the AI assistant or record spoken campaign feedback. Audio is converted into text in real-time for conversational intelligence. Background audio is NEVER monitored or recorded.

Document and verification data: copies of identity documents, NID or KYC documents, selfie verification images, business registration records, and related verification information when required for compliance or onboarding.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide and improve the SwapnoPay platform, process transactions, verify merchant identity, prevent fraud, support account operations, and maintain compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

We may also use personal data to send service notifications, payment confirmations, customer support updates, security alerts, security checks, and feature-related communications. If you opt in, we may use your information to send product updates or marketing messages.

3. Storage, Processing, and Security

We store data in secure environments with access controls, encryption in transit, role-based authorization, and audit logging. Sensitive information such as KYC records or payment data is handled according to security policies and applicable law.

We implement safeguards designed to reduce unauthorized access, data leaks, and misuse, but no system is completely immune to risks. You are responsible for protecting passwords and account credentials used with our services.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the platform, including payment processing, cloud hosting, analytics, anti-fraud checks, customer support, and compliance support. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, regulatory inquiry, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of SwapnoPay, users, or the public.

We do not sell personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may place cookies, local storage, or similar tools to remember preferences, understand traffic patterns, maintain sessions, and improve service performance. You can manage cookies in your browser settings, while noting that some features may not function optimally if disabled.

6. Merchant Verification and KYC

For compliance, access controls, and fraud prevention, we may collect and verify government-issued ID information, KYC documents, selfies, business registration data, and related metadata. This information may be reviewed internally or by approved external verification partners for risk assessment.

We retain verification data only for the period necessary for onboarding, legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal and transaction data for as long as required to provide our services, maintain records, comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it in line with our retention procedures.

8. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may have rights to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, depending on applicable law and jurisdiction. You may also object to certain processing or request restrictions on use where permitted. Contact us using the details below to exercise your rights.

You have the right to access, update, correct, or permanently request deletion of your personal data and account. You may also object to certain processing or request restrictions on use where permitted.

How to Delete Your Account and Associated Data

  • In-App Deletion: Log into the SwapnoPay Android app, navigate to More (আরও) tab, and tap Delete Account (অ্যাকাউন্ট ডিলিট). Confirm the deletion prompt to permanently remove your profile and stored store data.
  • Web Request Portal: If you cannot access the app or have uninstalled it, you can submit an instant deletion request at our dedicated portal: SwapnoPay Account Deletion Portal.

* Upon confirmation, profile information, KYC documents, and local ledgers are permanently purged within 30 days. Certain transaction records may be retained as mandated by applicable financial and tax laws.

9. International Transfers

Information may be transferred to countries outside your place of residence for hosting, payment support, analytics, customer support, or compliance operations. We use contractual protections and security controls designed to support safe international transfer where required.
